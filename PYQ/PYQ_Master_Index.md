For end-semester questions, revise **CLO5** and **CLO4** first because the EST papers repeatedly ask test scheduling, integration platforms, TLM, design methodologies and software design.

For mid-semester questions, revise **CLO1** and **CLO2** heavily because MST papers repeatedly ask SoC flow, ASIC flow, EDA tools, design metrics, chip basics, bus and NoC.
